# Thought Quieting

A guided, 20-minute Thought Quieting exercise for your phone, based on Exercise 25 in *THRIVEtoday, Track Two*
(Skill 2, quieting).

Open it: https://kohl-crypto.github.io/thought-quieting/

## Install (Android and iPhone)
The app shows the right steps for the phone it's opened on:
- **Android (Chrome):** tap **Install** on the home page, or ⋮ → **Install app**.
- **iPhone (Safari):** tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
- **Opened from Facebook, Messenger or Instagram?** Those can't install apps. Use ⋯ → **Open in browser** first.

To pass it on, use **Send this app to a friend** at the bottom of the home page.
Once installed it works with no signal and updates itself.

## The exercise
The point of the exercise is to catch a negative thought, then compare two ways of settling it:
arguing with it using logic, and talking with Jesus about it.

| Step | Time | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Get ready | 2 min | Read the steps; practice the RC nodding sequence |
| 2. Catch the thought | 2 min | One event or issue, the negative thoughts, and the conclusion you've drawn |
| 3. Talk back with logic | 3 min | Argue with the conclusion using reason |
| 4. Talk with Jesus | 5 min | Appreciation or time with Him; ask if the thoughts are true and how He sees it |
| 5. Breathe | 3 min | Slow belly breathing; relax |
| 6. Share | 5 min | Tell your partner how logic compared with talking to Jesus |

Each question appears on its own; when its time is up a soft chime plays and the next one appears.
If you're recording when time runs out, it waits for you to finish. Settings can switch to 4 or 5 minutes per step.

## Extras (each can be turned off under Extras)
- **Feeling check-in:** name the feeling (fear, anger, shame, disgust, hopeless despair, sadness) and where it sits
  in your body. The app suggests the feeling from your own words, and on the talk-back step points out absolute
  words you used, like "always" or "never".
- **How heavy? (0–10):** a quick score at the start, after logic, after Jesus and at the end. The summary shows which helped.
- **Background sound:** white noise, or a soft tone (174, 396, 417 or 528 Hz) suggested from the feeling you named.
  The tone pairings come from sound-healing tradition, not research. Keep it barely audible.
- **Record answers:** record or type what you say; your words are written out as you speak where the phone supports it.

The **mute** button (top right during the exercise, or Silent mode in Settings) turns off the voice, chime,
vibration and sound all at once.

## Privacy
Everything stays on the phone: answers in the browser's storage, recordings in its database. Nothing is uploaded.
Speech-to-text uses the phone's built-in voice typing, which on some phones is done by the phone maker's servers.

- `sw.js`: lets it work with no signal and updates it automatically.
- `manifest.json`, `icons/`: home-screen install.
