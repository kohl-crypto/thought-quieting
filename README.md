# Thought Quieting

A guided, 20-minute Thought Quieting exercise for your phone, based on Exercise 25 in *THRIVEtoday, Track Two*.

Open it: https://kohl-crypto.github.io/thought-quieting/

## How it works
1. Tap **Start**. The first question appears (and is read aloud, if that's on).
2. When the step's time is up, a soft chime plays and the next question appears by itself.
   If you're in the middle of recording, it waits until you stop.
3. On the steps that ask something, tap **Record my answer** if you'd like to.
   Your words are written out as you speak, where the phone supports it, and you can edit them or type instead.
4. At the end you get a summary of what you said, with your recordings, and how heavy the thought felt before and after.
   Copy or share it with your partner.

The six steps: get ready, what's on your mind, talk back with logic, ask Jesus for His view, breathe, share.
Timings follow the book (2, 2, 3, 5, 3 and 5 minutes); Settings can switch to 4 or 5 minutes for every step.

## Privacy
Everything stays on the phone: answers in the browser's storage, recordings in its database. Nothing is uploaded.
Speech-to-text uses the phone's built-in voice typing, which on some phones is done by the phone maker's servers.

## Install
- Android (Chrome): open the link, tap ⋮, then **Install app**.
- iPhone (Safari): open the link, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.

## Files
- `index.html`: the whole app.
- `sw.js`: lets it work with no signal and updates it automatically.
- `manifest.json`, `icons/`: home-screen install.
